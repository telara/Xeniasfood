About

A chef for those who care about quality and locality

Xenia is a private chef living between London and Wiltshire, cooking beautiful seasonal menus for discerning hosts who want the evening to feel effortless — and the food to be unforgettable.

From four-course dinner parties to canapé receptions, patisserie and bespoke weekly cooking, every menu is designed around your tastes, your guests and the best produce available.

Culinary Training

Trained at Ballymaloe Cookery School

Xenia's approach to food is rooted in the philosophy she learned at the world-renowned Ballymaloe Cookery School in County Cork, Ireland — the birthplace of modern Irish cuisine and a pioneer of farm-to-table cooking.

At Ballymaloe, chefs are taught to cook with the seasons, respect exceptional produce, and bring warmth and generosity to every plate. It is this foundation — combined with professional training at Orchards Cookery — that shapes Xenia's private chef work today.

"Simple, seasonal, ingredient-led cooking — done with care, precision and heart."
